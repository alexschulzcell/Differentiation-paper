# 03_lineage_calibration — Can the 18 differentiation models even reach their own lineage?

The positive-control question, asked before any programme result counts: does a data set's own differentiation contrast move the textbook markers of the axis it claims to model? Only 2 of 18 pass at the data-set level (7 of 14 at donor-cell resolution).
